📖 What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, damage, or theft.

In today's connected world, almost everything is online — banking, shopping, communication, and even health records. This makes it essential for everyone — not just IT professionals — to understand basic cyber hygiene and stay safe online.

⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails or websites trick you into revealing passwords, credit card numbers, or other sensitive information. Always verify the sender before clicking any link.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software (viruses, ransomware, spyware) that infects your device to steal data or cause damage. Avoid downloading files from unknown sources.

💻 Hacking

Unauthorized access to systems or networks to steal data, disrupt services, or plant backdoors. Keep software up to date to patch security vulnerabilities.

💳 Online Fraud

Scammers pose as banks, sellers, or officials to steal money or personal information. If an offer looks too good to be true, it probably is.

🔓 Weak Passwords

Short or obvious passwords can be cracked using automated tools. Use long, unique passwords and a password manager.

📶 Public Wi-Fi Risks

Unsecured public networks allow attackers to intercept your data. Use a VPN when connecting to public Wi-Fi.

✅ Basic Safety Tips

🔑 Use passwords with 12+ characters — mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
🔄 Never reuse the same password across different websites.
📲 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
🔗 Check for 'https://' before entering personal info on any website.
💾 Keep your OS, browser, and apps updated to fix security bugs.
📧 Do not click suspicious links or download unexpected email attachments.
🔒 Lock your devices when not in use and set strong PINs.
📦 Back up important data regularly to an external drive or cloud.
